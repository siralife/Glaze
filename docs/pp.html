Privacy Policy

• Delivery information

• Payment-related information

• Device, browser, IP address, and website usage information

The information collected by GLAZE may be used for the following purposes:

• To improve our website, products, and services

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember preferences, understand website usage, and maintain website functionality. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, government authority, court order, or where necessary to protect our legal rights, users, customers, or the security of our services.

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including order processing, customer support, accounting, legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and other legitimate business purposes.
