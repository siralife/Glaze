Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Glaze perfume last? +
Glaze perfumes are designed for long-lasting performance. Depending on your skin type, weather and application, the fragrance can last for several hours.
Are Glaze perfumes for men or women? +
Glaze offers versatile unisex fragrances, that can be enjoyed by both men and women.
How can I make my perfume last longer? +
Apply perfume to moisturised skin and focus on pulse points such as the neck and wrists. You can also lightly spray it on clothing from a suitable distance.
How can I contact Glaze? +
If you have any questions about your order, product or fragrance, please contact our customer support team through support@myglaze.in.
